Fajitas Recipe

Recipe Description:

These fajitas are a quick and easy meal option, featuring marinated chicken or beef, sautéed peppers, and onions, all wrapped in warm tortillas. Serve with your favorite toppings!

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb chicken or beef, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 4 tortillas
  • Optional toppings: sour cream, guacamole, salsa

Instructions:

  1. In a large skillet, heat some oil over medium-high heat. Add the sliced meat and cook until browned.
  2. Add the sliced peppers and onions, and sprinkle with fajita seasoning. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  4. Assemble the fajitas by placing the meat and vegetable mixture on a tortilla and adding your favorite toppings.
