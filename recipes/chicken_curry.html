Chicken Curry Recipe

Recipe Description:

This chicken curry is a delicious and flavorful dish made with tender chicken pieces simmered in a rich and aromatic curry sauce. Serve it with rice or naan for a complete meal.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, heat some oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
  2. Add the pureed tomatoes and cook for a few minutes until the mixture thickens.
  3. Stir in the curry powder and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they are browned on all sides.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk and bring the mixture to a simmer.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cover and let it cook for 20-25 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through.
